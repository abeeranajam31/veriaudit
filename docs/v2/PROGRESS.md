## Session 2 finding
The protocol's "≥ 80% power at n = 200 for a 10 pp gap" assumed scenario outcomes are observed
without error. Simulating disagreement among the 3 sampled responses gives 0.80 (high consistency),
0.76 (moderate) and 0.41 (low) for the primary test. The claim is now conditional, and the
calibration pilot must estimate response consistency and report the minimum detectable effect.
A draft change making the signed-rank test on shares primary was reverted to the pre-specified
McNemar test: under the planning model it had no power advantage (0.71–0.78 vs 0.76–0.80).

## Scientific limitations (current)
- v0.1 findings rest on 30 intents, 2 models, LLM-assisted labels for 72.5% of responses.
- Weapons category under-filled (14 of target 25) after excluding explosives/CBRN.
- Seeds are English-origin benchmark prompts; AdvBench items remain template-like even after deduplication.
- Rule judge evaluated in-sample; classifier baseline tiny.
